Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you deliver?

A standard SEO blog article is delivered within three days. Shorter edits go out in two, longer research reports take about five.

Do you use AI tools?

Yes, openly — as accelerators, not as substitutes for judgement. On writing they speed up research; every draft is then fact-checked, restructured and rewritten so it holds up to scrutiny. On build work they speed up the code; every tool is then tested against figures I work out independently before it goes anywhere near you. The three calculators on this page were built exactly that way, and their arithmetic is on screen for you to attack. What you are paying for is the judgement, the testing and the accountability — those are mine.

What about revisions?

Quick services include one revision within seven days. Standard writing packages include two rounds within the written scope. The quote specifies any different allowance. If a deliverable still misses the agreed scope, we discuss correction or refund under the service terms.

Can I see samples first?

Yes. Review a published article, our free browser tools, and the small-service scopes before sending a brief. Your quote states the exact deliverables for your order.

Do you build mobile apps for the App Store or Play Store?

No. Store publishing, review cycles and signing certificates are a different trade and I would be learning on your money. What I build runs in a browser — which on a phone looks and behaves like an app, can be saved to the home screen, and needs no store approval. If you genuinely need a native app, say so and I will tell you straight away that this is not the right place.

Who owns what you build?

You do — outright. You receive the source files, not just the finished output, and you are free to hand the project to any other developer the day after delivery without asking me. Nothing is hosted on my account and nothing depends on me staying available.

What if it breaks after delivery?

Fourteen days of free fixes on anything that does not behave the way the written scope says it will. After that, changes and new features are quoted separately — and I will always tell you when something is a five-minute fix you can do yourself.

What format does my data need to be in?

Digital and exportable: Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, a text-based PDF, or a database export. Scanned pages, photographs of forms and handwritten registers cannot be read reliably — anything derived from them carries errors that are hard to spot and expensive to find later. If that is all you have, tell me and I will quote the manual work honestly rather than pretend a machine can do it.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

Never, and please do not offer. Where a service needs an account in your name — hosting, a spreadsheet, an email platform — you create it and add me, or you sit with the screen for the two minutes it takes. Your logins stay yours.
