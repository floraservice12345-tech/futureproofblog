Questions before your first order

Can I order only one part of a larger project?

Yes. Ask for one resume section, one email, one article section, one chart or a small spreadsheet fix. For larger work, ask for a quote that breaks the deliverables and payment milestones into phases.

When do I pay, and is payment taken here?

The enquiry form takes no payment. We send payment instructions after you accept the written scope and quote. Small tasks are paid before work begins; larger projects may use an advance and final balance. Final editable files for staged projects are released after the agreed balance is confirmed.

What happens if I need a change?

Your written quote confirms the included revisions and how long you have to request them. The quick-task packages shown above include one revision requested within seven days. See our correction and refund policy for the applicable process.

What source files should I send?

Mention the files in your brief, then email editable DOCX, XLSX, CSV, a Google document link or a PDF with selectable text to contact@futureproofblog.in. Please avoid scans, passwords, identity documents and card details.

How is AI used in the service?

AI may assist with drafting, research or building tools, and a person reviews the deliverable. We use the facts you confirm and agree the scope before work starts. Services do not guarantee jobs, sales or rankings.
