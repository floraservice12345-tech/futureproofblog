Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary: We use newsletter details to send requested emails and enquiry details to respond to service requests. We do not sell personal data or turn a service enquiry into a newsletter subscription.

1. Who We Are

FutureProof Blog ("we", "us", "our") is an independent blog at futureproofblog.in that provides AI, career and personal finance insights for Indian professionals. Contact us at: contact@futureproofblog.in

2. What Data We Collect

The information depends on what you choose to do on the site:

  • Newsletter: the email address you submit and any name you provide.
  • Service and contact forms: your name, email, selected service, answers about your project, and any CV text, business details or links you choose to include. Files sent later are handled as part of that enquiry or order.
  • Site use: device, browser and traffic information collected by hosting, analytics and advertising services, and cookies described below.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use this information to:

  • Send the newsletter or guide you requested and manage your subscription.
  • Read an enquiry, clarify scope, prepare a quote, deliver an accepted service, and handle revisions, payment records or support.
  • Understand site traffic and improve pages and services.
  • Display and measure advertising where enabled.

Submitting a service enquiry does not sign you up for marketing emails. We do not sell or rent personal data. Service providers receive only information needed for the functions described here.

4. Your Choices and AI-Assisted Work

Newsletter emails require your sign-up and include an unsubscribe option. For an enquiry, we use the details you submit to respond; you can decide whether to accept a quote. If an accepted job needs a third-party AI tool to process client material, we will identify the tool and discuss the material to be shared before using it. Please do not put passwords, identity numbers, bank details or unrelated confidential information in a public form.

5. Data Retention

Newsletter records remain while your subscription is active; an unsubscribe stops future marketing emails. Enquiry and order records are kept while needed to respond, provide the service, resolve a dispute or meet applicable record-keeping duties. You may ask us to correct or delete information we hold, subject to any records we must retain. Contact us through the contact form or the address below.

6. Cookies and Advertising

FutureProof Blog uses the following cookies:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics may use cookies and related identifiers to measure traffic and page use.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense may use cookies to show relevant advertisements based on your browsing. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue) — For newsletter and transactional email delivery and enquiry contact handling. Brevo Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — For site traffic measurement. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — For advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Netlify — For hosting and storing website form submissions. Netlify Privacy Policy
  • AI tools selected for an accepted job — For drafting or analysis when the written scope calls for them. We identify the provider and discuss client material before sharing it. Provider terms and privacy practices may differ.
  • Unsplash — For images. Unsplash Privacy Policy

You can contact us to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion, subject to applicable obligations.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

Use our contact form or email contact@futureproofblog.in. We will respond to requests under applicable law.

Our services and enquiry forms are intended for adults. If someone under 18 needs to contact us about a service, a parent or guardian should do so.
