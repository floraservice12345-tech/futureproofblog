Frequently asked

Does this app call ChatGPT or Claude?

No. It creates a detailed prompt in your browser. Paste it into an AI tool you already use; this app needs no model key or usage fee.

Can content guarantee more customers?

No. Results depend on your offer, audience, service quality and response. The paid pack supplies ready-to-review copy, not a promise of reach or sales.

Will you publish posts or manage my ad account?

No. The standard service is a content calendar and copy pack. Posting, graphics, ads and account access are excluded.

How is my information handled?

Prompt-builder details stay in your browser. Optional Google Analytics runs only after your consent. If you submit the form, its fields go to Netlify so the provider can respond; form values are not sent to Google.
