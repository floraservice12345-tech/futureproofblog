Start with a small, clear brief

Tell me the goal, audience, source material, required file format and deadline. You receive the proposed scope, total price and delivery date before payment. Work starts after the agreed payment is confirmed.

For data tasks, send editable digital files such as XLSX, CSV, DOCX or selectable-text PDF. Scans, photos, image-only PDFs and handwritten records are not accepted.
