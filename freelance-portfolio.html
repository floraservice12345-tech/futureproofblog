A few useful details

What files should I send?

Editable, exportable XLSX, CSV, DOCX, Google Sheets or a PDF with selectable text. Scans, document photographs, image-only PDFs and handwritten records are not accepted. Start with a redacted example when asking for a quote.

Can I request a resume from scratch or a full article?

Yes. The larger services enquiry form covers resume writing from confirmed career facts, complete articles, reports and web tools. The quote sets the exact scope; starter prices above cover only the listed small tasks.

How is AI used?

AI may assist research, drafting, analysis and implementation. Outputs need review against supplied facts and the written brief. For a particular job, tell us your AI-use policy and confidentiality requirements before sharing the files.

What happens if the work misses the agreed brief?

Raise the specific issue so it can be corrected within the written scope. Revision and refund options are described in the quote and our paid-service terms. Rankings, job offers and revenue results are not promised.

I found you through Freelancer.com. Where should we discuss the project?

Use your existing Freelancer.com conversation for project discussion, agreement and payment. The public examples here demonstrate the work; a project introduced through Freelancer should follow its platform rules.
