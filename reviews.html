How reviews are handled

1

Nothing publishes itself. Every review arrives privately first. It goes live only after it has been read.

2

Bad reviews get published too. A two-star review that explains what went wrong is more useful to the next reader than ten five-star ones, and it tells me what to fix.

3

Nothing is written for you. Wording is never suggested, supplied, or “polished” into something you did not say. The only edit is trimming a very long review, and never in a way that changes what it means.

4

Reviews are never paid for. No discounts, no free work, no gift cards in exchange for a rating.

5

You can take it down. Email through the contact page and your review is removed, no reason needed.

6

Your email stays private. It is not published, not added to the newsletter list unless you sign up yourself, and not shared with anyone.
