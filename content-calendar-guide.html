Small business social media content calendar · India

30-day social media content calendar for Indian small businesses

A useful calendar alternates helpful advice, product context, proof you can verify and clear invitations to enquire. This plan works as a starting point for a neighbourhood shop, service provider or independent professional. Adapt each prompt to your real offer, language and audience.

Use only facts you can confirm. Do not invent prices, discounts, customer reviews, certifications or results. This plan can help you post consistently; it cannot guarantee reach or sales.

Before you schedule

  1. Choose one audience, such as nearby families, first-time buyers or returning customers.
  2. Write down the current offer, service area, hours and contact method you are comfortable publishing.
  3. Pick one main channel. Rework each post to fit the platform instead of copying identical text everywhere.
  4. Choose a realistic rhythm. Daily posting is optional; the 30 prompts can be spread across more than a month.

Your 30-day content plan

  1. Introduce the business and the area you serve.
  2. Explain one common question customers ask.
  3. Show a product or service in use, with permission where people appear.
  4. Share a short checklist that helps a customer choose well.
  5. Describe how to enquire, book or visit.
  6. Answer a second frequently asked question.
  7. Invite followers to ask a question; reply during your stated business hours.
  8. Explain how your service works from first contact to delivery.
  9. Show materials, preparation or a behind-the-scenes step.
  10. Share an honest comparison guide without attacking competitors.
  11. Introduce a team member or the owner and their role.
  12. Explain a local seasonal consideration relevant to your customers.
  13. Clarify who the service is for and who may need a different option.
  14. Repeat your contact and location details clearly.
  15. Share a customer comment only with permission and without changing its meaning.
  16. Give a simple care, maintenance or usage tip.
  17. Show two available options and explain the verified difference.
  18. Correct a common misconception about your category.
  19. Describe what information a customer should have ready before enquiring.
  20. Invite followers to save a practical checklist.
  21. Show an example of the finished work, with client approval.
  22. Explain delivery, appointment or service-area limits accurately.
  23. Share a short tutorial in the language your audience uses.
  24. State the current price or quote process if it is confirmed and public.
  25. Answer a question collected from a real enquiry, without identifying the person.
  26. Share a verified update about hours, availability or a seasonal service.
  27. Describe one quality check you actually perform.
  28. Ask which of two useful topics followers want explained next.
  29. Summarise the main benefits and limitations of your offer.
  30. Invite interested people to enquire, book or visit with one clear next step.

Make each post specific

Replace general claims such as “best quality” with a detail you can verify: the material, process, service area, turnaround estimate or included step. A clear caption usually has one opening sentence, one useful detail and one next action. Add a local reference only when it helps customers recognise whether the service fits their area.

Use WhatsApp responsibly

Send promotional updates only to people who agreed to receive them. Keep messages useful, identify the business and provide an easy way to stop future updates. For questions from customers, reply directly and avoid adding people to groups without permission.

Review what works

Once a week, note which posts led to relevant questions, profile visits, saves or enquiries. Compare similar posts over several weeks before changing your approach. Reach and sales also depend on the offer, audience, service quality, timing and follow-up.
