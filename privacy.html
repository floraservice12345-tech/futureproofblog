Prompt builder

Business details entered into the prompt builder are used in this browser tab to prepare text. The tool does not send or save those fields.

Enquiry form

If you submit the enquiry form, your name, email, business details and brief are sent to Netlify so the provider can receive the request and reply. Do not include passwords, payment details, customer lists or sensitive personal information. Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage the requested service, or as required by law.

Optional Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-BXCSZN56NT only if you select “Allow analytics”. If you reject or make no choice, the analytics script is not loaded. When enabled, Google receives page visit and general browser/device measurement data. Form values and prompt-builder inputs are not sent to Google. Your choice is stored in this browser; clearing this site's storage resets it. See Google's privacy information.
