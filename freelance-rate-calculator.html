The reality check most calculators skip

A rate is only real if someone pays it. Two things decide whether yours holds:

1. Price the project, not the hour. Per-word and per-hour pricing pay you for typing. They quietly penalise the interview you did, the dataset you cleaned and the three paragraphs you deleted because they were filler. Use the hourly figure above to sanity-check a project quote — never to quote by.

2. Consider recurring work. A retainer can reduce repeated prospecting and onboarding compared with the same amount of one-off work, but its value depends on scope, payment reliability and your capacity.

If the number above looks impossible at your current band, the answer is not to work more hours. It is to change what you sell — a niche, a research promise, or a measurable outcome.
