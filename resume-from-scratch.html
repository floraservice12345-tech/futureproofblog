What to prepare

Your facts remain yours to verify. We do not invent employers, qualifications, dates, metrics or achievements. Do not include Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or unrelated sensitive information.

AI use and limits

AI may help organize and draft the text. A person reviews the wording, and you must confirm that every personal detail is accurate before using the resume. No resume service can guarantee interviews, shortlisting or employment.

Frequently asked questions

Can you write it if I have no work experience?

Yes. We can structure education, projects, internships, volunteering and skills you can demonstrate. We will not present coursework as employment.

Is this different from a resume rewrite?

Yes. A rewrite starts with an existing resume. This service begins with a questionnaire and builds the document from your confirmed information.

Does ₹2,999 include every type of resume?

It covers one standard resume for one target role and one revision. Executive, highly technical, bilingual or multiple-role work may cost more; you will receive the final quote before work begins.

Will the resume pass every ATS?

No one can guarantee that. We use a simple, readable structure and can align relevant terms to one job description without keyword stuffing.
