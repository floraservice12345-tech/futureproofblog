      About this checker

      Does the percentage mean my resume will pass an ATS?

      No. It is only the share of selected job-description terms that appear verbatim in your resume. Different systems parse documents differently, and employers evaluate context, evidence and fit—not a score from this page.

      Does the tool store or upload my CV?

      No. Analysis happens in your browser. This page does not send the text to a server, save it in browser storage or include it in analytics events.

      Should I copy every missing keyword into my CV?

      No. Add a term only if it accurately describes your skills or experience. Keyword stuffing and invented claims can hurt your application and your credibility.

      Why might a relevant skill be missing from the results?

      This lightweight checker compares exact words and common phrases. It does not understand synonyms, abbreviations or context. Read the job description yourself and use the tool as a prompt to review, not as a final verdict.
