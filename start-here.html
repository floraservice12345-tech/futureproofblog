Four paths from reading to doing

Career

Prepare a clearer job application

Use your real experience and one target role.

  1. Understand resume structureCheck readability, relevant evidence and the employer’s requirements.
  2. Compare your CV with a job descriptionA keyword-overlap aid, not an ATS score or a hiring prediction.
  3. Check the opportunity itselfReview the work arrangement and warning signs before sharing information.

Optional next step: Request a headline and summary rewrite.

Small business

Test one manageable income idea

Start with scope and costs before increasing volume.

  1. Define one service you can deliverUse the worked example and the downloadable scope card.
  2. Check price, costs and discountsCalculate a planning contribution with your own assumptions.
  3. Explore one route to customersCompare seller providers, settlement terms and return handling.

Optional next step: Organise a project in the free brief builder.

AI and spreadsheets

Turn a repeated task into a useful workflow

Start small, using data you are permitted to share.

  1. Plan a spreadsheet-to-app projectStart with one input table and a defined result.
  2. Download the starter tracker (CSV)Use fictional or non-sensitive examples while learning.
  3. Write a build briefSpecify inputs, outputs, acceptance checks and exclusions.

Optional next step: Request up to three spreadsheet formula fixes.

Writing and content

Create something your audience can use

A useful answer and a sustainable workflow come before volume.

  1. Plan four useful videosUse the examples, cost check and current eligibility sources.
  2. Build a research-and-edit routineCheck evidence, permissions and the final wording.
  3. Download the four-video plan (TXT)Turn the topic into a demonstration, a next step and a review question.

Optional next step: Request a scoped writing quote.
