1. General AI assistants

The single most useful category. One good assistant handles drafting, editing, summarising, translation, planning and analysis. If you buy only one AI subscription in 2026, buy this one.

Approximate paid pricing: roughly ₹1,700–₹2,200 per month for individual plans on the major assistants, with free tiers that are surprisingly capable.

How to choose: test the same real task on two or three of them for a week. Judge them on how well they hold a long brief, follow formatting instructions and admit uncertainty — not on which writes the prettiest first paragraph.

3. Writing quality and editing

The cheapest quality upgrade available to a freelancer. A consistent editing pass raises perceived professionalism more than a better first draft does. Look for a tool that corrects without flattening your voice, and that understands Indian English conventions.

Approximate paid pricing: ₹900–₹2,500 per month; free tiers cover basic correction.

4. SEO and content planning

Keyword research, competitor analysis and on-page scoring. Worth paying for only when someone is buying ranking outcomes from you. If you publish two articles a month, this is an expensive checklist.

Approximate paid pricing: entry plans commonly ₹3,500–₹8,000 per month, rising steeply with seats and credits.

India caveat: check the depth of Indian keyword data before committing — several excellent tools have thin coverage outside the US and UK.

How these picks are chosen

Every category here reflects tools used in real client work rather than a feature-sheet comparison. A tool earns a place by removing a recurring hour from a real workflow. Categories where a free tier is genuinely sufficient are marked as such, even where a paid alternative would be more profitable to recommend.

Prices move, free tiers tighten, and products get discontinued. Confirm the current price on the vendor’s own page before subscribing, and prefer monthly billing until a tool has survived three months in your actual routine.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum AI tool spend to work professionally in 2026?

Zero to start and around two thousand rupees a month to work comfortably. One paid general assistant covers drafting, editing, summarising and analysis. Add a second subscription only when you can name the recurring task it removes and the hours it saves each month.

Are the free tiers of AI tools good enough for client work?

For most drafting, research and design tasks, yes. The limits you hit are usage caps, slower access at peak times and missing conveniences such as persistent style memory, document uploads and team sharing. Start free and upgrade at the point where the caps interrupt paid work.

Are the links on this page affiliate links?

Some outbound links on this site may become affiliate links, which would pay the site a commission at no additional cost to you. That arrangement never determines which tools are listed or how they are described, and the affiliate disclosure page explains the policy in full.

How often should I review my AI subscriptions?

Every three months. List each paid tool and write down the specific task it saved you in the previous month. Cancel anything you cannot answer for. Subscription creep is the most common avoidable cost for freelancers and small teams.

Which single AI tool should a small Indian business buy first?

One general assistant on a paid individual plan. It handles customer emails, product descriptions, social captions, summarising documents and basic analysis. Specialised tools only make sense once a specific bottleneck is clearly identified and measured.
