7 Free AI Tools for India in 2026: Uses and Limits

By Geeta Yadav, M.C.A. & MBA📅 July 2026⏱ 12 min read
✍️ Geeta Yadav — M.C.A. & MBA | Founder & Lead Writer, FutureProof Blog Independent researcher covering AI tools, careers and personal finance for India's growing professional class. All content is fact-checked and editorially independent.

You can try useful AI assistants and creative tools without starting a paid subscription. What “free” includes differs by provider: models, feature access, regional availability and usage caps can change, and a free plan is not necessarily suitable for every professional task.

This guide compares seven well-known free entry points by the work they can help with and the limits to check. Provider plan pages were reviewed on 6 October 2026. This is a feature guide, not a benchmark test; check each provider’s current terms before relying on a feature or uploading sensitive information.

What to check before choosing a free AI tool

Free access can be capped by message volume, feature, model, file size or time window. Providers may change these limits, and features can differ by country, account and device. Check the linked plan page for the specific capability you need instead of relying on a model name or quota copied from an older article.

For low-risk tasks, a free plan can be enough to try a workflow. Always verify factual answers against original sources, and keep confidential client, financial, health and identity data out of tools unless you have checked the provider’s data controls and your obligations.

Seven free AI tools to compare

1. Google Gemini (Free Tier)

Gemini can help with drafting, brainstorming and working with supported files and media. The exact models, context capacity and tools available on a no-cost account can change; Google publishes current feature and usage details on its plans and limits page.

Useful for: General assistance, drafts, and tasks that benefit from Google product integration when available.

Check first: Current account limits, supported file types, regional feature access and how your data is handled.

Access: gemini.google.com.

2. Microsoft Copilot (Free)

Microsoft offers a free Copilot experience, while some Microsoft 365 integrations and work features require an eligible account or subscription. Microsoft explains the differences in its Copilot plan comparison.

Useful for: Everyday questions, drafting and web-assisted tasks; check whether the feature you want is part of the free experience.

Check first: Sign-in requirements, availability, usage limits and whether your workplace permits the account for company data.

Access: copilot.microsoft.com.

3. Meta AI

Meta AI can provide conversational help and, where enabled, may appear in Meta apps or on the web. Its availability, supported languages, features and usage limits can differ by country, account and app version; do not assume that a feature shown in a demo is enabled for every Indian user.

Useful for: Quick prompts and social-content ideas when it is available in an app you already use.

Check first: Availability in your account, current limits and privacy settings. Avoid sharing confidential information.

Access: meta.ai or check the Meta app you use.

4. ChatGPT Free

ChatGPT’s free plan supports general questions, writing, learning and other tasks, with access and limits that can change. OpenAI describes the current tools and usage limits in its Free Tier FAQ.

Useful for: First drafts, explanations, outlining and small coding or analysis questions.

Check first: Which tools are currently available, message limits and whether a task needs a paid feature.

Access: chatgpt.com.

5. Claude Free

Anthropic’s free plan can help with writing, summarising, coding and analysis. Models and usage limits vary by plan and may change; see Anthropic’s plan guide for current details.

Useful for: Drafting, editing and reasoning through text-heavy tasks.

Check first: Current message limits, supported features, account eligibility and regional payment options before upgrading.

Access: claude.ai — free with email registration.

6. Perplexity

Perplexity is designed around web search and provides links with many answers, which can make it a convenient research starting point. A citation is not proof that the source supports the answer: open the original page, confirm the date and context, and use primary sources for important claims. Its plan comparison describes current free and paid access.

Useful for: Finding source pages and getting an initial map of a topic.

Check first: Search and upload limits, source quality and whether the source is current.

Access: perplexity.ai.

7. Canva AI (Free Tier)

Canva provides design tools and some AI features, but the available tools and allowance depend on plan and can change. Canva’s AI access guide explains how usage allowances work.

Useful for: Social graphics, presentations and visual drafts.

Check first: Which feature is available on your plan, your remaining allowance and the usage rights for assets you plan to publish or sell.

Access: canva.com.

How to Maximise Free AI Tools as an Indian Professional

Strategy 1: Use Multiple Free Tiers Together

Choose a tool based on the task you need to finish, its current free allowance and your data requirements. Keeping a second option can help when a feature is unavailable, but different tools also return different answers; verify important facts independently.

Strategy 2: Save Your Best Prompts as Templates

Save a reusable prompt with the task, audience, format and constraints. Review the output and correct it against reliable sources instead of spending extra prompts asking the same broad question.

Strategy 3: Protect your data

Do not paste confidential work, client records, identity documents or unpublished material into a consumer AI tool until you have checked the provider’s data settings, terms and your organisation’s rules. Remove personal details when possible.

Strategy 4: Upgrade only after measuring a need

Before paying, record which free-plan limit blocks your actual workflow and how often it happens. Compare the current plan price, features and cancellation terms on the provider’s own site; prices and taxes may differ by country and billing method.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which free AI tool has no limits?

Do not assume any free tool is unlimited. Usage caps and included features vary by provider, account and time. Check the provider’s current plan page before depending on a specific allowance.

Can I use a free AI plan for business?

It depends on the provider’s current terms, the feature and your intended use. Read the terms and data controls before using generated work commercially or entering client information; review the final output for accuracy and rights issues.

Is a free AI tool enough for study?

It can help explain a concept, create practice questions or organise notes, but it can make mistakes. Confirm answers with course materials and primary sources, and follow your institution’s academic-integrity rules.

Are all these tools and features available in India?

Availability can vary by product, feature, account, device and location. Open the provider’s current plan page and check your own account before planning a workflow around a feature.

Our Recommendations

A practical starting point by task:
  • Drafts and explanations: compare ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Copilot on the same low-risk prompt.
  • Finding web sources: try Perplexity, then open and verify the original sources.
  • Visual drafts: compare Canva’s available AI tools and other editors; check current asset rights before publishing.
  • Meta app workflow: use Meta AI only if the feature is enabled for your account and suitable for the data involved.
