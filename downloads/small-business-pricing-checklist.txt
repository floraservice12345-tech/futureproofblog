EXAMPLE ONLY
Price 500; listed order costs 230; waste allowance 20; owner-time allowance 100 => adjusted contribution 150. Fixed costs 4500 / 150 => 30 identical orders. A 10% discount gives price 450 and contribution 100 => 45 orders, assuming unchanged costs and no other expenses or taxes. This is not a forecast or statutory profit calculation.
